## Sounds

The creature voices of the Age of Mammals are built from 172 recordings of living animals (University of Cincinnati Scholar@UC dataset 2, freesound 90, wikimedia commons 80). The recordings
are public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA; none is NC or ND. They were changed: cut, denoised, slowed,
pitch-shifted, time-stretched, filtered, layered, mixed and loudness-matched. The full list of recordings, authors,
licences, source pages and the voice families each one went into is `deeptime_cenozoic-sound-credits.md` in this jar.
